# Order Outcome Analysis

## 📌 Problem
Analyze final order outcomes to understand delivery success and failure patterns.

## 📊 Approach
- Counted total orders by order_status
- Calculated percentage distribution
- Measured delivery success rate

## 📉 Key Findings
- ~97% of orders are successfully delivered
- ~0.6% of orders are canceled
- Remaining orders fall into intermediate states like processing/shipped

## 🧠 Business Interpretation
- High delivery success rate indicates strong fulfillment operations
- Low cancellation rate suggests good order confirmation and customer intent

## 🚀 Recommendations
- Monitor small % of cancellations for root causes
- Track intermediate states to identify operational delays
