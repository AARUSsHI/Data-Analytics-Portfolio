# Conversion Analysis

## Business Question
Which channels and landing pages generate the highest quality leads?

## Approach
- Joined MQL and Closed Deals datasets
- Created conversion flag
- Calculated conversion rates

## Findings

### Origin Analysis
- Unknown origin leads converted at 16.29%
- Paid Search converted at 12.30%
- Organic Search converted at 11.80%
- Email converted at only 3.04%

### Landing Page Analysis
- Several landing pages exceeded 20% conversion
- High-volume landing pages maintained strong conversion performance

## Business Interpretation
- Paid and Organic Search appear to generate scalable high-quality leads
- Unknown attribution requires investigation
- Email appears ineffective for lead acquisition

## Recommendations
- Increase focus on Paid and Organic Search
- Audit lead attribution process
- Reevaluate email acquisition strategy
